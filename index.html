About

I am a Machine Learning and AI researcher based in Espoo, Finland. I recently completed my PhD at Aalto University, where my work focused on flexible Bayesian latent-variable models for interacting processes in healthcare time series.

My interests sit at the intersection of probabilistic modeling and applied problems: coupled hidden Markov models for bacterial colonization dynamics, hierarchical Gaussian processes for glucose and insulin response, sparse Kalman filtering for nonlinear systems, and most recently Ising-machine formulations for problems in wireless communications.

I enjoy building models that stay interpretable under uncertainty, and working on problems where the data is messy, coupled, and scientifically interesting.
